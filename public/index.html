4 Troubleshooting

  • Light went green and won't turn on: you held too long and it's in Lock Mode. Press the button 3 times quickly.
  • Stuck in program mode: while on, press the Void case at the pin hole right of the button. The light turns off.
  • Want the factory colors back: while off, hold 5 seconds and release on red.
  • Brightness changed: holding past 1 second while saving cycles brightness. Release as soon as the save registers.
